**1. 5 Core Types of Neural Networks**

- **MLP (Multi-Layer Perceptron):** Standard architecture (Input -> Hidden -> Output). Best for tabular data solving standard classification or regression tasks.
    
- **CNN (Convolutional Neural Network):** Spatial feature extractor. Go-to for image and video processing. _(Yeh wahi architecture hai jo tumhare lubricant oil degradation project mein visual diagnosis ke liye apply hota hai)._
    
- **RNN (Recurrent Neural Network):** Sequential data processor with internal memory. Ideal for NLP, time-series, or speech (LSTMs/GRUs).
    
- **Autoencoders:** Compresses input into a lower dimension (latent space) and reconstructs it. Used for dimensionality reduction and noise removal.
    
- **GANs (Generative Adversarial Networks):** A Generator network aur ek Discriminator network ek dusre ke against compete karte hain. Generates hyper-realistic fake images, deepfakes, or music.
    

**2. The Deep Learning Timeline (History)**

- **1950s (The Start):** Frank Rosenblatt invented the _Perceptron_ (single-layer neural network).
    
- **1969 (1st AI Winter):** Marvin Minsky proved Perceptrons cannot solve non-linear problems (like the XOR logic gate). Research funding crashed.
    
- **1986 (The Breakthrough):** Geoffrey Hinton introduced _Backpropagation_, allowing multi-layer networks to mathematically update weights and learn complex functions.
    
- **1990s:** Yann LeCun created early CNNs for zip-code recognition, but hardware limitations caused a 2nd AI Winter.
    
- **2012 (The Big Boom):** Hinton’s team built _AlexNet_, combining massive datasets (ImageNet) with GPU computing. They crushed traditional algorithms, starting the modern DL era.
    

**3. State-of-the-Art Applications**

- **Autonomous Systems:** Real-time object detection powering self-driving cars (like Tesla's autopilot).
    
- **Strategic Intelligence:** Google's AlphaGo defeating the human world champion in the highly complex game of Go (2016).
    
- **Generative AI:** B&W image colorization, automatic image captioning, and generating non-existent human faces via GANs.
    
- **NLP:** Seamless real-time text and speech translation across languages.
